Frequently asked

Common admission questions

Detailed FAQs per programme on each guide.

Is there any management quota or paid seat?
+
No. SIMSREE has zero management quota · no reserved seats · no payment seats of any kind. All MMS admissions are through the State CET Cell · Government of Maharashtra · strictly on merit. Report any agent who claims otherwise.
Where do I find the latest notification?
+
Each programme's notification PDF is on its admission page · also on the homepage news ticker and News & Announcements archive.
What documents do I need?
+
See the Downloads page for the current "Documents Required" PDF · also the Anti-Ragging affidavit and Gap Certificate affidavit.
Who do I call for help?
+
Vinayak Khanvilkar · 022 6151 0709 · admissions@simsree.org. Student helpers (4): AJINKYA · RUPAL · SOURABH · VISHAL — numbers on the Contact page.
Can I visit campus before applying?
+
Yes. Open Mon-Sat · 11am-7pm · 2 min from Churchgate station. Schedule via the Contact page for a 30-min walkthrough.
