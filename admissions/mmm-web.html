Eligibility

Who can apply.

Six criteria designed for working marketing professionals seeking weekend executive education.

01

Bachelor's degree

3-year bachelor's (or equivalent) from a UGC-recognised university · any stream accepted with marketing work-ex.

Any discipline
02

Minimum 50%

Minimum aggregate of 50% in bachelor's (45% for reserved categories defined by Govt. of Maharashtra).

45% for reserved
03

2+ years marketing work-ex

Minimum two years of full-time work experience in brand, growth, sales, agency, digital, retail, B2B marketing, or related roles.

Verified via payslips + ITRs
04

Employer NOC

No Objection Certificate from your current employer permitting weekend attendance at Churchgate for the 3-year duration.

Mandatory · format on Downloads
05

Entrance + interview

SIMSREE entrance test followed by a 20-minute panel interview covering brand sense, career trajectory, and motivation.

Held at Churchgate · weekday slot
⚠ Important

Zero management quota

No management quota, no payment seats, no agents. Cohort is selected purely on entrance + interview + work-ex strength.

Report agents to admissions@simsree.org
Admission process

How selection works.

A six-step flow optimised for working professionals · two weekday slots in total.

1

Register on the SIMSREE admissions portal

Create your profile · upload bachelor's, payslips, ITRs, ID proof, photograph, signature · pay the application fee.

simsree.org/admissions · ~30 min
2

Submit employer NOC

Upload the signed NOC from your current employer permitting weekend class attendance for the 3-year programme.

Format · downloadable PDF
3

SIMSREE entrance test

90-minute objective + analytical paper · marketing basics, consumer behaviour, English, logical reasoning.

150 marks · MCQ
4

Panel interview

20-minute panel interview by 2 faculty + 1 senior alumni · covers career trajectory, brand POVs, motivation.

Same day as entrance
5

Merit list · 60 seats allotted

Combined entrance + interview + work-ex score forms the merit list. Result is published within 2 weeks of the round closing.

60 seats · 3-year cohort
✓

Pay fees · weekend induction

Pay first-semester instalment online. Orientation Saturday in early August · classes start the following weekend.

First instalment · ~₹0.55L

FAQ

Common questions.

When does the MMM Executive cycle open this year?
+
Open now · check the official Notification PDF on the Downloads page for exact dates.
What's the entrance pattern?
+
SIMSREE entrance + interview · weekend classes at Churchgate.
Is there hostel accommodation?
+
SIMSREE is a day-scholar campus. Students typically rent in nearby Churchgate, Marine Lines, or Nariman Point areas. The institute does not maintain hostels.
Are scholarships available?
+
Government-of-Maharashtra fee reimbursements per category-wise EBC/SC/ST/OBC scheme · plus Best Student Awards funded by SIMAA and Simarthan. Check the Downloads page for current schemes.
Whom do I contact for queries?
+
Vinayak Khanvilkar · 022 6151 0709 · admissions@simsree.org.
