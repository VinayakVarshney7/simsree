Eligibility

Who can apply.

Six criteria a candidate must satisfy. Each is verified during the entrance and document upload stages.

01

Bachelor's degree

3-year bachelor's (or equivalent) in Mathematics, Statistics, Economics, Commerce, Engineering, or related quantitative discipline.

Quantitative streams preferred
02

Minimum 50%

Minimum aggregate of 50% in bachelor's (45% for reserved categories defined by Govt. of Maharashtra).

45% for reserved
03

SIMSREE entrance

SIMSREE entrance test · objective + analytical sections · quantitative aptitude, financial literacy, comprehension.

Held at Churchgate · ~2 hrs
04

GD + PI clearance

Group discussion and personal interview after the entrance. Shortlist is by combined entrance + GD-PI score.

Same day or next-day slot
05

CFP® pathway eligibility

Programme includes a structured Certified Financial Planner pathway · candidates must be open to the additional FPSB India curriculum.

Optional but recommended
⚠ Important

Zero management quota

SIMSREE has no management quota, no payment seats, and no agent appointments. All admissions are strictly merit-based.

Report agents to admissions@simsree.org
Admission process

How selection works.

SIMSREE-run end-to-end · entrance, GD, PI, and merit allotment all happen at Churchgate.

1

Register on the SIMSREE admissions portal

Create your candidate profile · upload photo, signature, category certificates, marksheets · pay the application fee.

simsree.org/admissions · May–Jun 2026
2

Appear for the SIMSREE entrance test

Two-hour objective + analytical test covering quantitative aptitude, financial literacy, English, and logical reasoning.

200 marks · MCQ · Churchgate centre
3

Shortlist for GD-PI

Shortlist of ~3× the seat strength (≈ 120 candidates) called for group discussion and personal interview.

Shortlist published within 10 days
4

GD + PI · same-day evaluation

30-minute group discussion + 15-minute personal interview by a faculty + industry panel. Scored on coherence, depth, motivation.

Held at Churchgate · weekday
5

Merit list · 40-seat allotment

Combined entrance + GD + PI score forms the merit list. Reserved-category candidates are listed per Govt. of Maharashtra rules.

40 seats · merit only
✓

Pay fees · join induction

Pay first-instalment fees online. Induction starts early August with the CFP® pathway orientation.

First instalment · ~₹0.95L

FAQ

Common questions.

When does the M.Sc. Finance cycle open this year?
+
Jun 2026 · check the official Notification PDF on the Downloads page for exact dates.
What's the entrance pattern?
+
SIMSREE entrance test (objective + analytical) + group discussion + personal interview. Official notification PDF published each May-July. Includes CFP® certification pathway.
Is there hostel accommodation?
+
SIMSREE is a day-scholar campus. Students typically rent in nearby Churchgate, Marine Lines, or Nariman Point areas. The institute does not maintain hostels.
Are scholarships available?
+
Government-of-Maharashtra fee reimbursements per category-wise EBC/SC/ST/OBC scheme · plus Best Student Awards funded by SIMAA and Simarthan. Check the Downloads page for current schemes.
Whom do I contact for queries?
+
simsree.edu@gmail.com · 022 6151 0709.
