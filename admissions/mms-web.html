Eligibility

Who can apply.

Five criteria a candidate must satisfy. Each is verified during CET counselling and document upload.

01

Bachelor's degree

Any recognised 3-year bachelor's degree (or equivalent four-year programme) from a UGC-recognised university.

All streams accepted
02

Minimum 50%

Minimum aggregate of 50% in bachelor's (45% for reserved categories — SC/ST/OBC/PWD/EWS as defined by Govt. of Maharashtra).

45% for reserved
03

Valid CET / CMAT score

A valid score in MAH-MMS-CET or CMAT for the relevant academic year. Score must appear in the State CET Cell merit list.

CET / CMAT · current AY only
04

Domicile / category

Maharashtra State (Home State) candidates and All-India candidates both eligible · seat matrix per State CET Cell rules.

85% MS · 15% AI quota
05

Age & medical fitness

Minimum age 21 on the date of admission. Medical fitness certificate at the time of joining.

21+ · medically fit
⚠ Important

Zero management quota

SIMSREE has no management quota, no payment seats, and no agent appointments. All admissions are strictly merit-based, routed through the State CET Cell, Govt. of Maharashtra.

Report agents to admissions@simsree.org
Admission process

How selection works.

The CET Cell publishes the merit list. Counselling decides the allotment. Document verification finalises the seat. Here's the full flow.

1

Register on the State CET Cell portal

Create your candidate profile at cetcell.mahacet.org with name, photo, signature, category, and bank details.

cetcell.mahacet.org · Apr–May 2026
2

Appear for MAH-MMS-CET / CMAT

Either the state CET (MAH-MMS-CET, conducted by State CET Cell) or a valid CMAT score. CMAT scores accepted as per the year's notification.

CET · 200 marks · 2.5 hrs · MCQ
3

Submit CAP (Central Allotment Process) preferences

Fill your preferred institutes in order. SIMSREE typically appears at the top for candidates aiming for a Mumbai full-time programme. Lock your preferences before the deadline.

CAP I → CAP II → CAP III rounds
4

Get allotted · document verification

If allotted SIMSREE in a CAP round, freeze the seat and report to SIMSREE for offline verification (originals + 2 photocopies + photos).

Verification at Churchgate campus
5

GD-PI counselling (institute-level)

SIMSREE conducts a group discussion + personal interview as part of counselling — used for shortlisting in some categories. Not a re-rank — your CET score remains the primary criterion.

~30 min total · Churchgate · weekday
✓

Pay fees · join induction

Pay first-instalment fees via the State CET Cell payment gateway. Induction week begins early August. Welcome to SIMSREE.

First instalment · ~₹1.15L

FAQ

Common questions.

When does the MMS cycle open this year?
+
May 2026 · check the official Notification PDF on the Downloads page for exact dates.
What's the entrance pattern?
+
State CET Cell · Government of Maharashtra · merit only · CAP rounds (Central Allotment Process) followed by ACAP (Against Vacant Seats). GD + PI as part of counselling.
Is there hostel accommodation?
+
SIMSREE is a day-scholar campus. Students typically rent in nearby Churchgate, Marine Lines, or Nariman Point areas. The institute does not maintain hostels.
Are scholarships available?
+
Government-of-Maharashtra fee reimbursements per category-wise EBC/SC/ST/OBC scheme · plus Best Student Awards funded by SIMAA and Simarthan.
Whom do I contact for queries?
+
Vinayak Khanvilkar · 022 6151 0709 · admissions@simsree.org · Mon-Sat 11am-7pm.
