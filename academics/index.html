Why SIMSREE academics

A programme designed for practitioners.

At SIMSREE, academic depth is non-negotiable. But it's the combination — rigour plus the student-driven committee system, the Churchgate location, and the active alumni network — that makes the SIMSREE graduate distinctive.

Industry-led teaching

Senior practitioners co-teach every elective. Faculty bring industry experience plus scholarship.

Case-based pedagogy

Real cases · real briefs · real consulting engagements. Theory is grounded in practice.

Outcomes-focused

100% placement · ₹16.5L average CTC · 120+ recruiters. Outcomes published, not promised.

Full-time programmes

Built for those who can give two years.

MMS and M.Sc. Finance are SIMSREE's full-time, on-campus master's programmes — two years of intensive learning at the heart of Mumbai's financial district.

MMS
Full-Time · 2 years · 120 seats

Masters of Management Studies

India's longest-running MBA-equivalent. Specialisations in Finance, HR, Marketing, Operations, Systems. Admission via Maharashtra CET.

  • 5 specialisations · Finance · HR · Marketing · Operations · Systems
  • 2-year structure · 4 semesters + summer internship
  • ~₹4.5L total fees · 4 instalments
  • State CET Cell · merit-only admission

Executive programmes
