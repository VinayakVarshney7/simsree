Specialisations

What you can specialise in.

Each specialisation has dedicated electives, faculty advisors, and industry mentor pairings.

Capital Markets

Equity research, trading, portfolio management, investment banking.

Banking

Corporate banking, retail banking, risk, treasury, regulatory.

Wealth Management

CFP® pathway · financial planning · advisory · private banking.

Fintech

Payments, lending tech, digital banks, blockchain, RegTech.

Curriculum

What you'll learn.

A blend of foundational management, electives in your specialisation, and capstone work — all reviewed against industry every two years.

Semester 1 · Foundations of Finance
+
Financial Accounting · Quantitative Methods · Microeconomics · Corporate Finance · Indian Financial System · Communication for Finance.
Semester 2 · Capital Markets & Banking
+
Investments & Portfolio Management · Banking Management · Financial Markets & Institutions · Macroeconomics & Monetary Policy · Derivatives.
Summer · Industry internship
+
8-12 week placement in BFSI · trading desks, equity research, banking, fintech, treasury.
Semester 3 · Advanced electives
+
Equity Research · Fixed Income · Risk Management · Mergers & Acquisitions · Fintech · International Finance · Behavioural Finance.
Semester 4 · CFP® pathway + capstone
+
CFP® certification preparation (per FPSB India authorised partner status) · capstone industry project · final placement cycle.
CFP® Partnership · FPSB India

SIMSREE is the Best Authorised Institutional Partner 2025 for FPSB India. The M.Sc. Finance includes a structured pathway to the Certified Financial Planner (CFP®) qualification — recognised globally and across India's wealth-management sector.
