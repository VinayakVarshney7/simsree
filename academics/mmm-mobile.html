Curriculum

What you'll learn.

A blend of foundational management, electives in your specialisation, and capstone work — all reviewed against industry every two years.

Year 1 · Foundations
+
Marketing Management · Consumer Behaviour · Managerial Economics · Statistics · Communication · Research Methodology.
Year 2 · Marketing depth
+
Brand Management · Sales & Distribution · Digital Marketing · Pricing · Service Marketing.
Year 3 · Strategy + project
+
Marketing Strategy · Marketing Analytics · International Marketing · Retail Management · capstone project at workplace.
