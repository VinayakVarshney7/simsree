The story

How edition 29 played out.

The first post-pandemic edition

The fest came back from a two-year hiatus.

After 2 years of remote-only editions, the 29th Simerations returned in-person. The first 100 delegate passes sold out in 48 hours. Energy on Day 1 was unlike anything we'd seen on campus.

Day 1 · 11am

Four parallel tracks, all rooms packed.

Finance Forum, Marketing Mavericks, Consulting Crack, Ops & Supply Chain — four tracks running simultaneously. 398 delegates across 32 B-schools. Every chair filled by 10:55am.

Day 2 · The Pivot

XLRI Jamshedpur · Team Pivot · the grand prize.

XLRI's Pivot team turned a near-disaster Round 1 (third-place finish) into a Round 2 reversal. They built a Consulting × Marketing crossover deck that became the case study for the next year's training material.
