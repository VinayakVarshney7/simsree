The story

How edition 30 played out.

Theme reveal

'Resilient Business' — a theme born in post-pandemic dust.

The 30th edition asked one question: how do businesses, leaders, and individuals come back stronger? Every track was framed around comeback strategy — turnarounds, pivots, second acts.

TEDxSIMSREE collab

A first-time joint opening with TEDxSIMSREE.

For the first time, Simerations and TEDxSIMSREE shared an opening keynote — 8 minutes per speaker, three speakers, three lenses on resilience. Format we'll keep going forward.

Day 2 · The Finale

Team Lakeside, the no-mentor underdogs.

NMIMS Mumbai's Lakeside team was the first in five years to win the grand prize without a single industry mentor on roll. A reminder that competition can still surprise.
