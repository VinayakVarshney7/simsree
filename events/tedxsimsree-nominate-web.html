Nominate a voice that should be heard.

Every November we share six talks. 200+ nominations come in each cycle. We read every single one. The 2025 closing speaker arrived via an Instagram DM. The bar isn't credentials — it's an idea you cannot stop thinking about.

How it works

Three steps. One read.

There is no committee filter. Every nomination is read by two curators. Strong nominations get a 30-minute call within 14 days.

Step 01

Suggest the person

Their name, what they do, and where we can see them speak (YouTube, LinkedIn, podcast, panel — any clip works).

Step 02

Describe the idea

In 2–3 sentences. The one they keep coming back to. The talk they would give if we said yes.

Step 03

We reach out

Strong nominations get a 30-min call within 14 days. If we move forward, we co-write the talk over 8 weeks.

Curation lens

Four things tip a nomination.

01

A specific idea, not a topic

"AI in healthcare" is a topic. "Why diagnostic AI gets it wrong 40% of the time at rural clinics" is a specific idea. We pick the second every time.

02

First-person evidence

The speaker has lived inside the question. They have data, a body of work, a personal stake. Not just a strong opinion.

03

Surprise, not just smarts

The idea should challenge how someone in the audience already thinks. If everyone already agrees, it is a panel, not a talk.

04

Willing to coach

We rehearse for 8–12 weeks. The speaker must be willing to rewrite, cut, and rewrite again. Brilliance without coaching becomes a podcast.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Can I nominate myself?

Yes, but the bar is higher. Use the same form. The "Your relationship" field is where you say so. Self-nominees get the same 14-day response time.

What if my nominee is not in Mumbai?

That is fine. We fly speakers in. About 40% of our 2024 lineup was non-Mumbai. The cost is on us, not the speaker.

Do you only consider Indian speakers?

No. We have featured speakers from the US, UK, Singapore, Kenya, and Vietnam. The license is independent of nationality.

How long is the talk?

9 to 14 minutes. No exceptions. The format is part of the discipline.

Will the talk be filmed?

Yes — by Plexure Films. The speaker keeps the rights to the video and gets the masters. We post it on our YouTube channel.
