The story

How edition 31 played out.

Day 1 · 12 Sept

The opening keynote that set the tone.

Reema Mahajan opened with a sharp keynote on the next decade of finance — capital allocation, AI in markets, the retail-investor wave. Standing ovation. The fest had its theme before the first round even kicked off.

Day 1 · 11am-5pm

Round 1 across 5 parallel rooms.

Finance Forum at the auditorium · Marketing Mavericks in Hall 2 · Ops & SCM in the Lab · Consulting on the rooftop · Founders' Studio in the courtyard. 482 delegates, 5 simultaneous tracks, zero hiccups.

Day 2 · The Finale

Team Visionary, the night-before pivot.

IIM Lucknow's Visionary reshuffled their entire deck at 11pm Day 1 after spotting a gap in the consumer-research data. Day 2's panel saw it instantly. Standing ovation in the auditorium when results were announced.
