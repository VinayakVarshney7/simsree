FAQ

Common questions.

Who can attend an MDP?

MDPs are open to mid- and senior-level professionals (3+ years of experience). Career Catalyst workshops are for early-career professionals and MBA students. Custom in-company MDPs are tailored to your team\'s level.

Are programmes hybrid or in-person?

Most MDPs run on the SIMSREE campus in Mumbai. Selected programmes also have a hybrid option with live-streamed sessions; the practical labs and group exercises are in-person only.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes — every MDP and Career Catalyst programme issues a SIMSREE certificate of completion. Attendance ≥80% is required.

Can my employer sponsor me?

Yes, most participants are sponsored. We can issue a GST invoice in your employer\'s name on registration. Email mdp@simsree.org for the brochure deck.

Group discounts?

Yes — 10% off for groups of 3-5, 15% off for 6+. Talk to the MDP Office before registering.

Need something different?

A custom MDP for your team.

50% of our MDPs last year were delivered as private programmes for specific organisations. Tell us what you need, we will build it around your team and your timeline.
