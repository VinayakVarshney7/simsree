About Simerations

India's most well-organised B-school fest. We're going to prove it again.

Simerations is SIMSREE's flagship national management fest, run end-to-end by 80+ students across the Placement, Events, Marketing & Finance committees. Since 1993, 32 editions, 14,000+ alumni delegates.

This year's theme — Future-Forward Finance — frames the entire fest around the next 5 years of finance: AI in capital markets, the great Indian retail-investor wave, and ESG mandates that are about to reshape the corporate balance sheet.

FAQ

Questions before you commit.

Who can register?

Any current MBA / PGDM / equivalent student from a recognised b-school in India. Some tracks also welcome final-year undergrads from BBA, BMS, Economics streams. Mixed teams across colleges are allowed but each member must be a current full-time student.

What's the registration fee?

Per-team registration is ₹1,500 for Finance, Marketing, Consulting and Ops tracks. Founders' Studio is ₹2,000 per applicant and includes 2 months of post-fest mentorship regardless of final ranking. Group bundles (3+ teams from same college) get 15% off.

Is travel / stay covered?

For teams shortlisted to the finals (Day 2), we cover one night of accommodation near campus (Mumbai). Travel reimbursement is at the discretion of the Travel Committee — students from 100+ km out who reach Round 2 get a ₹2,500 travel grant.

Can I attend as a delegate without competing?

Yes — Day 1 keynotes, Day 2 founders' studio pitches, and the workshop sessions are open-attendance with a delegate pass (₹500). Includes lunch + the industry mixer.

Is everything in-person?

Tracks 1-4 are in-person at SIMSREE Churchgate. Founders' Studio pitches and the Day 1 keynote are also livestreamed via TEDxSIMSREE's YouTube channel.

What about prior years' editions?

2025 was won by Team Visionary from IIM Lucknow. 2024 by Team Pivot from XLRI. Browse the full archive at the bottom of this page or scroll up to pick another edition.
