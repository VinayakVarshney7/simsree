What we do

Three things, done seriously.

01

The annual event

One afternoon every November · six speakers · a 320-seat auditorium · livestream to 4,000 more on YouTube. Curated, rehearsed and produced by a 22-person student crew.

Past editions →
02

Speaker curation

200+ nominations a year, narrowed to 6. We rehearse with each speaker for 8-12 weeks, give script feedback, and refuse to let anyone read off a deck. Talks are about ideas, not credentials.

Nominate a voice →
03

A year-round community

Salons, watch-parties, idea-circles · all year. We host conversations on campus and in Mumbai cafés where the talks of the year are taken apart, picked at, and built on.

Who we are

A student-run TEDx, independently organized, in Mumbai.

TEDxSIMSREE is operated under license from TED. The license is held by the SIMSREE Student Council; the work is done by a 22-person committee that turns over every academic year. No faculty advisor curates the talks. No one gets paid. The standard we hold ourselves to is the same TED holds itself to: curiosity, reason, wonder, and the pursuit of knowledge — without an agenda.

History

From a hostel room in 2016 to the auditorium today.

2016

The license arrives.

Three first-years apply for a TEDx licence after attending TEDxGateway. They get it on the second try. No money. No venue. A November deadline.

2017

The first edition · "Unbound".

Six speakers · 180 seats sold · zero corporate sponsors. Filmed by a friend with one camera. The closing talk got 800k views in 90 days.

2020

The skipped year.

We chose not to hold a virtual edition during lockdown — felt wrong. We came back in 2021 with "Lighthouse", streamed-only, from a closed auditorium.

2025

The seventh · "Crossroads".

320-seat sold out in 11 minutes · livestreamed to 14 partner colleges · cumulative talks past 11M YouTube views. The standard we set in 2017, held.
