Stand alongside ideas that matter.

TEDxSIMSREE is independent, student-run, and unmistakably visible. Brand partners cover production · enable livestream · fund scholarships for 30 visiting student delegates a year. We do not pre-package units. We build the partnership around what your organization wants to associate with.

Partnership tiers

Three lanes. One conversation.

These are starting points, not packages. The final partnership is co-designed in a 45-minute call with our Marketing & Partnerships lead.

Title partner
₹8L+
One per edition. Your brand is the headline. "Presented by [you]" across every surface — stage, video, social, post-event reels.
  • Headline placement on all stage assets & YouTube reel
  • 10-min industry segment between two talks (optional)
  • 20 reserved seats in the auditorium
  • 3-page brand spread in printed programme
  • Year-round talk-license usage for internal training
Anchor partner
₹3L+
Up to four per edition. Strong brand presence across one of: stage design · livestream · printed programme · scholarship fund.
  • Anchor placement on chosen surface
  • Branded scholarship for 7 visiting student delegates
  • 10 reserved seats & green-room access
  • Branded social-post drop in talk-release schedule
  • Aftermovie credit + behind-the-scenes feature
Track partner
₹1L+
Brand associated with one of six committee tracks (eg. Marketing partner backs the Marketing & Media track). Operational support, not stage time.
  • Track-lead introduction at all-hands kickoff
  • Internship pipeline to 4 committee members
  • 4 reserved seats
  • Programme + post-event press credit
  • Annual case-study collaboration
