Run the 2026 edition with us.

Twenty-two students run TEDxSIMSREE every year — from curating speakers to running the AV desk on show day. Applications for 2026 open March 1. The application is read in 72 hours. If you get a callback, you get a real seat at the table.

The team

22 students. Eight months. One afternoon.

The committee turns over every academic year. No faculty advisor curates the talks. The team is built to give each member real ownership over a slice of the event.

Open roles · 2026

Six tracks. Pick one.

You apply to a track. Each track has 3-5 seats. Track lead is a returning second-year — they coach you.

01

Curation

3 seats · 8hr/week

Read every nomination. Run 30-min discovery calls. Shape the year's theme. Decide which six speakers make it.

Skills you bring: writing · pattern-spotting · politeness under pressure
02

Speaker coaching

4 seats · 10hr/week from July

Each member coaches one speaker through 8 weeks of rehearsals. Script feedback, structural rewrites, stage direction.

Skills: storytelling · script-doctoring · diplomatic feedback
03

Production

5 seats · 6hr/week, full days on show week

Run the auditorium, the livestream rig, sound, lighting, run-of-show, green room. The single biggest team.

Skills: logistics · staying calm · vendor management
04

Design

3 seats · 6hr/week

Brand the year. Build the website, social grid, programme booklet, on-stage motion graphics, signage.

Skills: Figma · motion · type · brand thinking
05

Marketing & Media

4 seats · 6hr/week

Build the audience. Manage social. Pitch to press. Cut the aftermovie. Run the talk-release plan post-event.

Skills: writing · video · platform sense · press contacts
06

Operations

3 seats · 5hr/week

Hold it all together. Finance, partnerships, delegate registrations, hospitality, post-event reporting.

Skills: spreadsheets · email · talking to grown-ups
Timeline · 2026 cycle

From application to showtime.

1 March

Applications open

Form goes live on this page. 14-day window. We read every one.

15 March

Applications close · interviews begin

Shortlisted candidates get a 30-minute call with the current track lead.

5 April

Team announced

22 seats filled. First all-hands kickoff that weekend on campus.

May–July

Curation & planning

Curation finalises the lineup. Production scouts vendors. Design builds the brand. Marketing seeds.

August–October

Coaching & build-out

Speakers rehearse weekly. Tickets open. Production load-tests AV. Last 6 weeks are full-throttle.

14 November

Showtime

Doors at 14:00 · curtain at 15:00 · closing at 19:30 · after-party at Marine Drive.
