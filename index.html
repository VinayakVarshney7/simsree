Why SIMSREE

The distinguishing factors.

A management education that combines academic rigour, real-world exposure, and the entrepreneurial spirit of India's financial capital.

Academic Rigour

Excellence in every classroom.

No matter what you study, the future demands both depth and breadth. So does SIMSREE. Our programmes push students beyond theory into practice — case-based teaching, live projects, and a curriculum refreshed against industry every two years.

Explore academics
Industry-Led Learning

Practitioners, not just professors.

Learn from the people who do the work — SIMSREE brings in the best minds from India's corporate world, week after week. CXO guest lectures, live consulting briefs, and corporate visits aren't extras. They're the curriculum.

Meet our faculty
Outcomes

Careers prepared for, not promised.

Top recruiters. Strong alumni network. An active placement cell that connects your skills to the right opportunities — from internship to offer. The Placement Committee, like every committee here, is run by students themselves.

Placement outcomes
Location advantage

Anchored in Mumbai's financial core.

Located at Churchgate — steps from the Bombay Stock Exchange, the Reserve Bank of India, and hundreds of corporate headquarters — SIMSREE doesn't just teach business. It practises it.

This proximity is our advantage. Our students walk into guest lectures with CFOs, internships at trading desks, and careers at the firms they studied in class.
