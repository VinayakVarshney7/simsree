How placement works

Student-driven · faculty-guided.

The Placement Committee — an officially constituted student body — runs every step under the Placement Officer and the Director.

01
Day 0 · 45-60 min

Pre-Placement Talk

Company arrives on campus and briefs the cohort about the role, the mandate, the team and the growth track. Q&A baked in.

Run by: Placement Committee
02
Day 1 · 24-hr window

Resume Scrutiny

Interested candidates upload resumes on the Placement Portal. The Committee normalises every CV to the recruiter's format and ships within 24 hours.

Owner: Placement Portal · PlaceComm
03
Day 2 · 90 min

Group Discussion

Shortlisted candidates discuss in 8-10 person groups per the recruiter's chosen format — case-based, abstract, or current affairs.

Moderated by: Placement Officer
04
Day 3 · 30-45 min/slot

Personal Interview

Company runs 1:1 interviews with shortlisted candidates. Recruiters frequently use a 2-3 round panel for senior roles · multiple offers possible.

Slots managed by: PlaceComm
05
Day 3 EOD

Final Selection

Results announced same-day. Candidate accepts or declines within the window agreed with the recruiter. Multi-offer fairness rules apply.

Verified by: Placement Officer
06
Within 2 weeks

Final Offer

Recruiter issues the formal offer letter. Compensation, joining date and location confirmed. Crisil-audited and recorded in the official placement log.

Audited by: Crisil

The placement journey

Six steps · fully transparent.

Every interaction with a recruiter follows the same student-led, faculty-mentored process. No black box.

1
Pre-Placement Talk

Company arrives on campus · briefs the cohort about the role, mandate, team and growth track. 45-60 minutes including Q&A.

2
Resume Scrutiny

Interested candidates upload resumes via the Placement Portal · Placement Committee normalises to the recruiter's preferred format and ships within 24 hours.

3
Shortlist

Recruiter shares shortlist by midnight 2 days before the slot. Candidates get individual SMS + email notifications by 9am the next morning.

4
Aptitude / Case · optional

Roles requiring quantitative aptitude or case-solving get a 75-90 minute on-campus written/online round. Most consulting and finance recruiters use this stage.

5
Group Discussion

5-7 candidate GDs, each 25 minutes. Faculty observers join for first-time recruiters. Results inside 90 minutes.

6
Personal Interviews + Offer

2-3 round PIs · ending the same day with offer rollouts in the auditorium. Average time from PPT to offer: 5.4 hours.
