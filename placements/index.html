How placement works

Student-driven · faculty-guided.

The Placement Committee — an officially constituted student body — runs every step under the Placement Officer and the Director.

1

Pre-Placement Talk

Company briefs students on roles and projects.

2

Resume Scrutiny

Resumes sent to company for shortlisting.

3

Group Discussion

Shortlisted candidates discuss per company process.

4

Personal Interview

Company conducts individual interviews.

5

Final Selection

Final results announced.

6

Final Offer

Offer made to selected students.
