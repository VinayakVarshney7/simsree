Campus Life · A SIMSREE story

It's not a building.
It's a rhythm.

Scroll through a day in our life at Churchgate — from 6am laps at Marine Drive to midnight committee huddles in the canteen.

Scroll
The numbers

A campus that punches above its postcode.

SIMSREE sits inside a 1936 heritage block of Sydenham College — 2.4 acres in Churchgate, walking distance from the BSE, NSE, RBI, and the head offices of half the Nifty 50.

01

Inside the financial mile.

The address says it all: B-Road, Churchgate. From the front gate, it's a 4-minute walk to the BSE, 7 to the NSE, 9 to the RBI. Most of the Nifty 50 hold their head offices inside this single square mile.

This isn't a backdrop. It's the curriculum. The CFO you study on Monday might walk into the auditorium on Wednesday. The brand whose case you crack might call you for a live consult by month-end.

02

A 1936 heritage block.

SIMSREE shares its building with Sydenham College of Commerce — India's oldest commerce college (est. 1913) and one of Mumbai's listed Grade-II heritage structures. Stone arches, mosaic flooring, four-meter ceilings.

The 2.4-acre campus is fully walkable in 6 minutes. Inside: 14 classrooms, two auditoriums, a library, the placement floor, three labs, two canteens.

03

Five minutes to the sea.

Walk out the back gate, cross one signal, and you're at Marine Drive — the Queen's Necklace, three kilometres of unbroken Arabian Sea promenade. 6am runs. 11pm walks. The decompression chamber of SIMSREE.

Approximately every successful piece of student work since 1983 has its conception story on that promenade.

A day in the life

From 6am to midnight, the campus moves.

06:30

Coffee on the steps.

Apsara opens at 6. Cohort regulars are there by 6:30 — cortados, last-minute case-prep on iPads, the day's Mint opened to the markets page. By 7:45 the steps are full.

10:00

Class is in session.

First lecture from 10am. The Finance section is in Room 203, Marketing in 117, Operations in the new wing. Tea break at 11:45. Lunch at 1.

15:00

3pm guest speaker.

Every Wednesday at 3pm a senior industry leader is on campus. This week: the CFO of an NSE-listed firm. Next week: head of equity research at a global bank. Last week: a VC partner from Lightspeed.

19:30

Committee night.

13 student committees, all running in parallel. Finance Forum at the library, Placement Committee on the second-floor recruiters' floor, Marketing & Media in the courtyard. Pizza from Hotel Pritam Da arrives at 9.

23:00

Marine Drive.

After class, after committee work, after dinner — there's the sea. Friends, problems, pitches, breakups, breakthroughs — they all happen on this 3-kilometre promenade.

The facilities

Nine spaces, each one a different mode.

⦿

The Library.

32,000 volumes · 240 journal subscriptions · Bloomberg + Refinitiv terminals · open till midnight during placement season. Quiet wing on the east side, group-discussion wing on the west.

⦿

The Auditorium.

320-seat heritage auditorium with original 1936 acoustic geometry. Hosts the Wednesday guest lecture, every TEDxSIMSREE edition, and the annual Simerations finals.

⦿

The Labs.

Three labs: a 40-seat trading lab with live BSE/NSE feeds, a marketing-analytics lab with Tableau + Power BI licences, and an operations lab with simulation software for supply-chain modelling.

⦿

The Canteen.

Run by the original family that's served Sydenham since 1962. Vada-pav at ₹15. Filter coffee at ₹20. The single most important node in SIMSREE's information network.

⦿

The Courtyard.

Open central courtyard with banyan trees, stone benches, and 4G everywhere. Where committees meet at 7pm, where strategy sessions happen between classes, where Simerations sets up its registration desk.
