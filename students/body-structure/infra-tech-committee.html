About this committee

What the Infra Tech Committee does

Infra-Tech runs SIMSREE's digital backbone — AV systems for events, the website CMS, photo and content moderation across committees, and the campus tech stack.

AV & Event Tech

Run sound, lighting, projection and streaming for every campus event.

Website Operations

Maintain the institute's website — content updates, photo approvals, broken links.

Campus Tech Support

Manage the computer lab, projector inventory and committee Drive structure.
