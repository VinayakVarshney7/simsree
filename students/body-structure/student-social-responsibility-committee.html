What the SSR Committee does

The SSR committee runs Mrudgandha — SIMSREE's flagship community outreach — alongside awareness campaigns, NGO partnerships and on-the-ground volunteer drives.

Mrudgandha Initiative

Lead SIMSREE's flagship community-outreach programme through the year.

Awareness Campaigns

Plan campaigns around mental health, gender, sustainability and education.

Build long-term partnerships with 5+ Mumbai-based NGOs.
