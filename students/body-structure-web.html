The leadership ladder

From member to GS

Every student starts as a member. Two years later, the top end up as Chairpersons, Council members, or GS.

Year 1 · Member

Apply during induction · execute under chair guidance.

Year 2 · Chair

Lead the committee · own outcomes · mentor juniors.

Year 2 · Council

Cross-committee coordination · institute strategy.

Year 2 · GS

Apex student leadership · elected by the batch.
