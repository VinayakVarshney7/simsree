L'Oreal Brandstorm National Winners 2025
Team SIMSREE-Mavericks · MMS 2024-26 · ₹3L + Global Final invite

Team SIMSREE-Mavericks (3 MMS 2024-26 students) won L'Oreal Brandstorm National finals 2025 in Mumbai. Their pitch: a refillable haircare line targeting Tier-2 Indian salons. Beating 240+ teams nationally, they secured a ₹3L prize and invitations to the Global Final in Paris (June 2026).

Team: Aarav · Priya · RohitPrize: ₹3L + Global FinalBeat: 240+ teams
Scholarship · Awarded
Aditya Birla Scholarship 2025
Recipient · MMS 2024-26 · ₹4L per year

Sneha Reddy (MMS 2024-26) is a 2025 Aditya Birla Scholar — one of 36 selected nationally from 8,200 applicants. The scholarship covers ₹4L per year of fees and includes structured mentorship from Aditya Birla Group leadership.

Recipient: Sneha ReddySelection: 36 / 8,200Worth: ₹4L per year
Publication
ESG Disclosure in Indian Banks
R&C Club · IIMB Management Review · Aug 2025

A 14-page paper by SIMSREE's Research & Consulting Club on India's evolving ESG disclosure landscape was accepted for publication in the August 2025 issue of the IIMB Management Review. The paper analysed 50 listed BFSI firms across 3 years of disclosures.

Authors: 4 students + Faculty MentorSample: 50 firms · 3 yearsJournal: IIMB Management Review
Sports
Inter B-School Cricket Runners-Up
Indore · Feb 2026

SIMSREE Sports Cell's cricket team reached the finals of the 14-school Inter B-School Cricket Cup at IIM Indore, February 2026. After defeating XLRI, NMIMS, and IIM Lucknow en route, they lost the final to IIM Bangalore by 4 runs.

Team: 11 + 4 reservesRound: Runner-upBeat: XLRI · NMIMS · IIM Lucknow
External
FPSB India · Best Institutional Partner 2025
Awarded · Hyderabad · Nov 2025

The Financial Planning Standards Board of India awarded SIMSREE its Best Authorised Institutional Partner 2025 at a Hyderabad ceremony. The citation highlighted SIMSREE's high CFP completion rates and curriculum integration.
