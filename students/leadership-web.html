How leadership is elected.

Four-step process · runs in Week 4-8 of Year 2.

1 · Nomination + 5 endorsements
+
Self-nomination opens Week 4 of Year 2. Each candidate needs five batchmate endorsements to qualify.
2 · Manifesto + open debate
+
Each candidate publishes a 1-pager manifesto. A campus-wide moderated debate runs in Week 5.
3 · Secret ballot
+
Entire MMS batch eligible to vote. Single transferable vote per portfolio.
4 · 2-week structured handover
+
Outgoing leaders hand over portfolios across two weeks · transition docs, contacts, ongoing initiatives.
Selection for Chairpersons (per committee)

Three-step gate: Year-1 performance review by exiting chair → 5 peer endorsements → Director sign-off.
