Our story

A legacy built over four decades.

In keeping with the rich Sydenham tradition of imparting high quality education, the Sydenham Institute of Management Studies, Research and Entrepreneurship Education (SIMSREE) was established in 1983 — a natural extension of Sydenham College of Commerce, one of Mumbai's most celebrated educational institutions.

Located at Churchgate, in the heart of the financial capital of the country, SIMSREE has always benefited from being the cynosure of the corporate world and the opportunity to draw from the finest in the industry. This unique positioning has allowed the institute to attract eminent faculty and offer students unparalleled access to corporate India.

For over four decades, SIMSREE has emerged as one of India's premier management institutes, dedicated to producing global managers of shining excellence. The institute continues to uphold and deepen its founding commitment to academic rigour, entrepreneurial spirit, and a student-driven culture.

Campus & culture

Where learning goes beyond the classroom.

At SIMSREE, the campus is not a backdrop — it is part of the curriculum. From the moment students arrive, they are expected to lead, organise, connect, and create. The institute runs largely on student initiative through 13 active committees.
