A network that never stops working.

Alumni regularly return to campus as mentors, guest lecturers, and recruiters. The relationship between SIMSREE and its graduates is active, not ceremonial.

From senior bankers and media heads to entrepreneurs and public servants, SIMSREE graduates carry the institute's values — initiative, rigour, and integrity — into every role they hold. They conduct mock GDPIs, lead corporate interactions, participate in Batch Meets, and fund student recognition programmes.
