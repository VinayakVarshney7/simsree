Established · 1983

A legacy built
over four decades.

In keeping with the distinguished tradition of Sydenham College of Commerce, SIMSREE was founded in 1983 — at Churchgate, the very heart of Mumbai's financial district. Forty years on, the institute stands at the confluence of legacy and ambition.

Our advantage

Steps from India's financial core.

Churchgate places us minutes away from India's most important banks, stock exchanges, and corporate headquarters. This unique positioning has allowed the institute to draw from the finest industry talent and offer students unparalleled access to the corporate world.

Over forty years, SIMSREE has grown from a promising new institute into one of India's most respected management schools. What has remained constant is the founding commitment: student-driven learning, entrepreneurial thinking, and an uncompromising standard of academic excellence.

Purpose

Vision & Mission.

Two statements that have not changed since 1983.

01
Our Vision

To be a leader in management education.

Recognised and respected by the corporate world as an institution of academic excellence. To shape students into industry-ready professionals who can lead with confidence in a globally competitive environment.

02
Our Mission

Four commitments we make.

  • iLet student initiative drive the institution.
  • iiMaximise real-world exposure — internships, live projects, guest lectures, management events.
  • iiiFoster a culture of research, inquiry, and entrepreneurial thinking.
  • ivInstil discipline, integrity, and ambition to lead organisations toward lasting success.
Core values

What we stand for.

01
Excellence

02
