Our story

A legacy built over four decades.

In keeping with the distinguished tradition of Sydenham College of Commerce, the Sydenham Institute of Management Studies, Research and Entrepreneurship Education was established in 1983. Located at Churchgate — the very heart of Mumbai's financial district — SIMSREE has always benefited from its proximity to India's corporate and financial epicentre.

Heritage building photo · 1983
Our advantage

Steps from India's financial core.

Churchgate places us minutes away from India's most important banks, stock exchanges, and corporate headquarters.

This unique positioning has allowed the institute to draw from the finest industry talent and offer students unparalleled access to the corporate world. Over forty years, SIMSREE has grown from a promising new institute into one of India's most respected management schools.

What has remained constant is the founding commitment: student-driven learning, entrepreneurial thinking, and an uncompromising standard of academic excellence. The institute today is a confluence of legacy and ambition — preserving its heritage while pushing forward with new programmes, new partnerships, and new ideas.

Milestones

Defining moments.

From 1983 to today · the moments that shaped SIMSREE.

1983
SIMSREE established
Sydenham Institute of Management Studies, Research and Entrepreneurship Education founded — extending the legacy of Sydenham College of Commerce.
1990s
Executive programmes introduced
MFM (Master's in Financial Management) and MMM (Master's in Marketing Management) launched to serve working professionals.
2000s
Doctoral programme launched
Ph.D programme started to produce original research across management disciplines.
2010s
Consistent NIRF presence
Inclusion in NIRF national rankings · continued growth of placement outcomes and alumni network.
2024
Dr Homi Bhabha State University · Cabinet approval
State Cabinet approves SIMSREE's integration into Dr Homi Bhabha State University · the next chapter of the institute's structure.
Nov 2025
FPSB India · Best Authorised Institutional Partner 2025
Awarded the Best Authorised Institutional Partner 2025 by the Financial Planning Standards Board India · ceremony held in Hyderabad.
2026
M.Sc. Finance & MMM/MFM AY 2026-27 cycles in progress
Active admission cycles across all five programmes · MMS opens via Maharashtra CET.
Purpose

Vision & Mission

Two statements that have not changed since 1983.

Our Vision

To be a leader in management education — recognised and respected by the corporate world as an institution of academic excellence. To shape students into industry-ready professionals who can lead with confidence in a globally competitive environment.

Our Mission

  • Let student initiative drive the institution.
  • Maximise real-world exposure through internships, live projects, guest lectures, and management events.
  • Foster a culture of research, inquiry, and entrepreneurial thinking.
  • Instil discipline, integrity, and ambition to lead organisations toward lasting success.
Core values

What we stand for.

Five values that guide every decision at SIMSREE.

Excellence

An uncompromising standard across academics, research, and student experience.

Collaboration

Faculty, students, alumni, and industry working together — not in silos.

Integrity

Zero management quota · merit-only admissions · transparent decision-making.

Wisdom

Knowledge that is rigorous, relevant, and applied to real problems.

Empathy

Leadership grounded in social responsibility — Mrudgandha and beyond.

Initiative

The institute runs largely on student initiative · responsibility is the curriculum.
