Entirely student-driven

A culture that's distinctive.

"Tell me and I'll forget. Show me and I may remember. Involve me and I'll understand." — the guiding philosophy at SIMSREE since 1983.

SIMSREE runs largely on student initiative. Committees are not clubs — they are the engine of the institute. Students negotiate with corporates, manage budgets, coordinate events, and run the placement process. By the time they graduate, they have not just studied management. They have practised it.
