The system

Not a student body.
An engine.

SIMSREE runs largely on student initiative. Committees are not clubs — they are the operational core of the institute.

Students negotiate with corporates, manage real budgets, coordinate large-scale events, and drive the entire placement process. By the time SIMSREE students graduate, they have not just studied management. They have practised it — for two full years, at scale, with real consequences.

This is what makes the SIMSREE graduate different. Other MBA programmes teach about leadership. SIMSREE assigns it.

Spotlight

What committees actually do.

Three signature committees · three real stakes.

Spotlight 01

Placement Committee

The Placement Committee doesn't just schedule interviews. It manages the entire student-to-recruiter pipeline — from JD curation and company relations to offer tracking and salary data.

Students who run this committee graduate with negotiation, project management, and stakeholder management skills that most MBAs only teach in year three.

Spotlight 02

Entrepreneurship Cell

Ideas are cheap. Execution is the curriculum.

The E-Cell runs pitch competitions, startup bootcamps, and connects student founders with industry mentors. It is the reason SIMSREE has been called an institute that doesn't just teach entrepreneurship — it creates entrepreneurs.

Spotlight 03

SSR Committee

Great managers understand more than markets. SIMSREE's SSR committee runs Mrudgandha and other community initiatives, ensuring that every student develops a social conscience alongside a professional one.
