Simarthan.

An independent, not-for-profit organisation dedicated to the advancement of education, training, and research. As SIMSREE's committed institutional partner, Simarthan works to ensure that the institute's mission endures, grows, and reaches further.

Purpose before profit

Why Simarthan exists

Founded by SIMSREE alumni and well-wishers who recognised the need for a dedicated body to champion the institute beyond government and university structures.

The name reflects that purpose. Simarthan combines SIMSREE with arthan (Sanskrit for purpose). Simarthan operates independently with a single focus: strengthening SIMSREE and its students.

Six objectives

What Simarthan does

Aligned to SIMSREE's mission.

Advance education

Improving the quality and reach of management education at SIMSREE.

Fund scholarships

Student awards, research, academic prizes through alumni contributions.

Industry links

Internships, live projects, placement infrastructure.

Infrastructure

Technology, facilities, learning resource upgrades.

Alumni ties

Bridging current students with the wider alumni network.

Events

Seminars, MDPs, conferences alongside academic calendar.

Get in touch

Connect with Simarthan

For partnerships, sponsorship, research co-funding.
